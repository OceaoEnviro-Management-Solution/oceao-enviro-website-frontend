# Oceao Enviro

## 📌 Project Overview

Oceao Enviro is the official digital platform being developed to strengthen the company's online presence and provide centralized access to environmental and regulatory information.

The project is being developed in a phased manner to ensure scalability, maintainability, and long-term sustainability.

---

## 🎯 Current Objectives

- Develop a professional corporate website.
- Build an automated Government Regulatory Updates Portal.
- Establish a scalable foundation for future development.

---
